# Code of Conduct

Irwin contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces welcoming, respectful, and focused on constructive technical discussion.

Examples of behavior that supports this standard include:

- Using respectful language and assuming good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Giving actionable, specific feedback about code and ideas rather than attacking people.
- Respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Accepting maintainer decisions and discussing concerns in the appropriate project channel.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to Wilbert Yang at **[zxpiayru@gmail.com](mailto:zxpiayru@gmail.com)** with the subject `Irwin conduct report`. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community; retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable. Do not use public issues to disclose security vulnerabilities; see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for private security reporting.
